"But, what if…?"

The 8 questions we hear every day. Your questions, our straight answers.

Is there a commitment?

Move and Form include a 6-month commitment; Limitless includes a 1-year commitment. To discover SVB without commitment, choose the €30 Pass Try or the €99.90 Pass Starter, each available once per person. SVB Boost at €12.90/month is an add-on to an active membership and is not compatible with Limitless.

How do I cancel a session?

You can cancel up to 1 hour before class, directly from the SVB app, and recover your credit.

Pause during holidays?

Yes, for an absence of at least 10 days, with 1 month's notice. With medical evidence, the membership can be paused without notice.

How does direct debit work?

Memberships are paid monthly. Registration fees and commitments are shown clearly on the Memberships page. Cancellation is requested by email with 1 month's notice, subject to the commitment period.

Is it for me, even as a beginner?

Absolutely. All our classes have options for beginners. Coaches adjust the Reformer springs and cardio intensity to your level. The €99.90 Pass Starter includes 5 different sessions of your choice, with no commitment.

How does the €30 Pass Try work?

For €30, you get two sessions in two different disciplines, available once per person. The registration fee is waived if you join right after either trial, or reduced by 50% within 3 days after the final trial.

Where are you? How do I get there?

Two studios in Saint-Ouen, both 4 minutes walk from Mairie de Saint-Ouen metro (lines 13 & 14). Parc des Docks Studio (Small Group) at 6 Mail André Breton. Cours des Lavandières Studio (Reformer) at 40 Cours des Lavandières. Parking nearby.

Injury or pregnancy: what to do?

Ask a healthcare professional first and tell the coach before class. Options may be offered when exercise has been medically cleared.
