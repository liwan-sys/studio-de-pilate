"But, what if…?"

The 8 questions we hear every day. Your questions, our straight answers.

🔓

Is there a commitment?

Yes, our Small Group memberships include a 6-month commitment. But we also offer a no-commitment option from €12.90 for those who prefer to start at their own pace, without pressure.

⏱️

How do I cancel a session?

You can cancel up to 1 hour before class, directly from the SVB app. No penalty, no credit lost. Other studios require 24 hours — not us.

🏖️

Pause during holidays?

Yes. Your membership pauses when you're away (holidays, work travel, injury). Just let us know 1 month in advance (except for members on a commitment plan). You don't pay for sessions you don't take.

💳

How does direct debit work?

Automatic monthly payment, same amount every month, charged on the 1st. Transparent, secure, cancellable at any time through your bank. No hidden fees.

💪

Is it for me, even as a beginner?

Absolutely. All our classes have options for beginners. Coaches adjust the Reformer springs and cardio intensity to your level. The Starter Pass at €99.90 (5 sessions, no commitment) is designed for this.

✨

How does the €30 trial work?

You book your session at €30 at one of our 2 studios. Monthly offer: if you subscribe within 7 days, the €30 is fully deducted from your first payment (standard: €15 deducted). No credit card required at booking.

📍

Where are you? How do I get there?

Two studios in Saint-Ouen, both 4 minutes walk from Mairie de Saint-Ouen metro (lines 13 & 14). Studio Breton (Small Group) at 6 Mail André Breton. Studio Lavandières (Reformer) at 40 Cours des Lavandières. Parking nearby.

🤍

Injury or pregnancy: what to do?

Talk to us. Our coaches are trained in prenatal Pilates and post-injury adaptation (partner physios available if needed). Classic Pilates, gentle Vinyasa Yoga and Core & Stretch are specifically adapted.
