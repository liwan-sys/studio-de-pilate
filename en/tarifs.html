8 questions we hear every day

Common questions before choosing a membership — with clear answers.

💳 Is SEPA direct debit safe? +

Yes. SEPA is a standard European bank mandate. Your IBAN stays with you, you can revoke authorization anytime from your banking app, and you have 13 months of dispute rights if there's an error. It's more secure than card payments for recurring subscriptions.

📅 Why a 6-month commitment? +

The 6-month commitment is designed to support a regular routine. It includes small-group sessions, the applicable holiday-pause terms and flexible cancellation up to 1 hour before class. After 6 months, you can cancel by email with 1 month's notice or continue.

⏱️ How do I cancel if something comes up? +

At SVB you can cancel or reschedule a session up to 1 hour before class from your account. Your credit is automatically added back to your pass for another session this month.

🌊 Do I pay if I go on vacation? +

No. Let us know before you leave by email or WhatsApp and we pause your membership while you're away. You resume when you get back, with nothing charged.

🔄 Can I roll over unused sessions to next month? +

No, unused credits are lost at month end — this is how we keep our prices low and seats available. Choose the right volume (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 sessions) for your real rhythm, and use the 1h cancellation rule to never lose anything to surprises.

📆 If I start mid-month, do I pay the full month? +

No, we prorate it based on when you start. The exact amount to pay today is shown automatically in the member area when you pick your plan.

🔒 Are there hidden fees? +

No. The only additions: filing fee at sign-up (amount shown in the member area, reduced with SVB Boost Option), a small extra fee if you pay by card instead of SEPA, and bank guarantee — activated only if you miss a payment, never charged by default.
