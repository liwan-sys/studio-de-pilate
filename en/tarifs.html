8 questions we hear every day

What blocks 9 out of 10 people before signing up — and our honest answers.

💳 Is SEPA direct debit safe? +

Yes. SEPA is a standard European bank mandate. Your IBAN stays with you, you can revoke authorization anytime from your banking app, and you have 13 months of dispute rights if there's an error. It's more secure than card payments for recurring subscriptions.

📅 Why a 6-month commitment? +

Because real results require consistency. 6 months is the minimum time for your body to change, build habit, and escape the "sign up / quit" cycle. In return, we guarantee small group sessions, free vacation pause if you're away, and flexible 1-hour cancellation. After 6 months, you can cancel via email with 1 month notice, or continue freely.

⏱️ How do I cancel if something comes up? +

At SVB you can cancel or reschedule a session up to 1 hour before class from your account. Other studios in the area require 24h. Your credit is automatically added back to your pass for another session this month.

🌊 Do I pay if I go on vacation? +

No. Let us know before you leave by email or WhatsApp and we pause your membership while you're away. You resume when you get back, with nothing charged.

🔄 Can I roll over unused sessions to next month? +

No, unused credits are lost at month end — this is how we keep our prices low and seats available. Choose the right volume (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 sessions) for your real rhythm, and use the 1h cancellation rule to never lose anything to surprises.

📆 If I start mid-month, do I pay the full month? +

No, we prorate it. Example: you take a 4-session Reformer Pass at €136.30, start mid-month and plan 2 sessions before month end — you pay €68.15 for that first month, then €136.30/month after. The calculator above shows your exact amount.

🔒 Are there hidden fees? +

No. The only additions: filing fee at sign-up (€49 for small groups, €25 for Kids) — cut in half with SVB Boost Option (€12.90/month). +€0.30 if paying by card instead of SEPA. And bank guarantee — activated only if you miss a payment, never charged by default.

🎁 Can I gift a membership or pass? +

Yes, our SVB gift cards work with custom amounts or pre-set plans (Reformer trial €30, Starter Pass €99.90, session packs...). Very popular for Christmas, birthdays, and weddings. See gift cards →
