Reformer

Pilates Reformer

50 min · 9 max · all levels. Adjustable springs, controlled movements and progressive deep-muscle work. Tell the coach about any limitation; seek medical advice before training during pregnancy or when returning after an injury.

Flow Reformer

9 people maximum. A more fluid, dynamic Reformer class with minimal pauses to develop strength, mobility, balance and coordination.

Before your first class in Saint-Ouen.

Where can I take yoga classes in Saint-Ouen-sur-Seine?

SVB Yoga Vinyasa and Cross Yoga classes take place at Parc des Docks Studio, 6 Mail André Breton, 93400 Saint-Ouen-sur-Seine, with up to 12 participants. Classes last 60 minutes and beginners are welcome. Cours des Lavandières Studio is dedicated to Reformer and Crossformer.

How much does yoga cost and how can I try it?

Check SVB prices and introductory offers to compare packs and memberships, their validity and conditions. SVB Move is the membership for Parc des Docks classes. You can check available sessions before choosing a plan.

How do I book yoga in the evening or at the weekend?

Open the SVB timetable, choose Parc des Docks Studio and then Yoga Vinyasa or Cross Yoga. Times and available places depend on the current timetable. Contact the team if you need help choosing your first class.

Where can I take Pilates Reformer classes in Saint-Ouen-sur-Seine?

SVB Pilates Reformer and Flow Reformer take place at Cours des Lavandières Studio, 40 Cours des Lavandières, 93400 Saint-Ouen-sur-Seine. Each participant has their own machine, with up to 9 people per class. Reformer sessions last 50 minutes, with options for beginners.

How much does Reformer cost and which plan should I choose as a beginner?

The SVB pricing page lists introductory offers, packs and memberships with their conditions. SVB Form is the membership for Reformer and Crossformer at Cours des Lavandières. Compare plans and their commitment periods before booking.

How do I find a time and book my first Reformer class?

Open the SVB timetable, choose Cours des Lavandières Studio and then Pilates Reformer or Flow Reformer. Check availability for your preferred day. Contact the team for help choosing a first class.
