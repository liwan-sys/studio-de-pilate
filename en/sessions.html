Crossformer

Crossformer

50 min · 8 max · all levels. SVB's signature format combines Reformer movements with dynamic cardio phases. The coach adjusts the springs, pace and options during each session.

Crossformer Challenger

8 people maximum. A more intense version with sustained sequences that further challenge strength, endurance, core stability and body control.

🌱

Reformer

Pilates Reformer

50 min · 9 max · all levels. Adjustable springs, controlled movements and progressive deep-muscle work. Tell the coach about any limitation; seek medical advice before training during pregnancy or when returning after an injury.

Flow Reformer

9 people maximum. A more fluid, dynamic Reformer class with minimal pauses to develop strength, mobility, balance and coordination.

Cross & strength

Cross Training

12 people maximum. A playful full-body class built around varied circuits, mainly bodyweight exercises and loads adapted to your level.

Cross Rox

12 people maximum. HYROX-inspired strength, cardio and functional stations for discovering the discipline or preparing for an event, without completing a full HYROX.

Cross Core

12 people maximum. Complete exercises targeting the deep, superficial and oblique abdominal muscles throughout the session.

Cross Body

12 people maximum. No-impact full-body strength training designed to protect the back and knees.

Cross Yoga

12 people maximum. Fitness-focused yoga with no spiritual component, combining mobility, flexibility, balance, core work and strength.

Pilates, Yoga & mobility

Yoga Vinyasa

60 min · all levels. Dynamic flow where every movement follows your breath. You strengthen, you stretch, you sweat — and you leave aligned.

Classic Pilates

12 people maximum. Gentle, precise mat work for deep strength, posture, mobility and body control.

Power Pilates

12 people maximum. A more dynamic Pilates format combining strength, core stability and movement control.

Pilates Barre

12 people maximum. Pilates principles combined with barre exercises to strengthen deep muscles and improve posture, balance and mobility.

Stretch Mobility

12 people maximum. A gentle class focused on flexibility, mobility and range of motion to release tension and improve posture.
