7 ways to feel alive.

One method per goal

Tone, cardio, mobility, power, confidence. 16 certified coaches, small groups (max 12), technique first.

Crossformer

Crossformer

50 min · 8 max · all levels. SVB's signature class: 20 minutes of Reformer to activate deep muscles, then 15-20 minutes of dynamic cardio. The combination stimulates the EPOC effect (post-workout calorie burn). Many see visible progress after 8-12 weeks of regular practice.

🌱

Reformer

Pilates Reformer

50 min · 8 max · all levels. Calibrated springs, technical precision, zero joint impact. Most practitioners report better postural awareness from the first sessions, with progressive work on deep muscles. Accessible to everyone — pregnancy, postpartum, seniors, returning from injury.

⚡

Pass Cross

Cross Training

55 min · all levels. SVB's most versatile format: cardio + light weights + bodyweight. Push yourself without breaking — technique before speed.

Cross Rox

55 min · intermediate. Hyrox-inspired: running + functional workshops. You leave exhausted, you come back. For athletes who want a real challenge.

Cross Core

55 min · all levels. Deep abdominal strength, smart core work, obliques. We don't do 100 crunches — we do 20 exercises done right. Changes feel noticeable after a few weeks of regular practice.

Cross Body

55 min · beginner friendly. Full-body strengthening with no impact or jumping. Perfect for returning to fitness, postpartum, or protecting your joints.

Cross Yoga

55 min · all levels. Yoga + mobility, pure physical focus (no chanting, no spiritual). Ideal after a heavy Cross Training session or a tough Monday.

🧘‍♀️

Pass Focus

Yoga Vinyasa

60 min · all levels. Dynamic flow where every movement follows your breath. You strengthen, you stretch, you sweat — and you leave aligned.

Hatha Flow

60 min · beginner friendly. Slower practice, poses held 30-90 seconds. Perfect for beginners, regaining calm, or working on deep alignment.

Classic Pilates

55 min · all levels. Traditional mat-based Pilates. Movement control, breathing, Powerhouse. Technical foundation, no machines.

Power Pilates

55 min · intermediate. Mat Pilates at a faster pace — rapid sequences, moderate cardio. More sweat than Classic, no machines.

Boxing

55 min · all levels. Footwork, stance, combinations, evasion. Bags and pads — zero contact. Technique + cardio + confidence. Equipment provided.

Afrodance'All

60 min · fun. Choreography to Afro beats, group cardio, pure release. You sweat, you laugh, you enjoy. Come in comfortable clothes.

Core & Stretch

55 min · all levels. Perfect mix: core work + guided stretching. Ideal after a big week, or to complement Reformer and Cross training.

Pass Kids

Training Kids

45 min · 5-12 years · Saturday 9:30 AM. Motor skills course, team games, sports challenges. We build coordination, self-confidence, and team spirit.

Yoga Kids

45 min · 5-12 years · Wednesday 4 PM. Learn to breathe, ground yourself, know your body — through stories and playful poses. Helpful for classroom focus.
